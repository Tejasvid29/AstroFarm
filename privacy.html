AstroFarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05

What AstroFarm is

AstroFarm is a personal, single-user tool for creating, managing, and publishing short-form astrology content. It produces short astrology videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel (Star Seeker). Where its owner has authorized it, AstroFarm may also connect to the owner's TikTok account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's explicit consent, AstroFarm uses the YouTube Data API (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) for the owner's own channel only, to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, schedule and visibility;
  • check the publishing status of those uploaded videos.

What it stores and shares

  • AstroFarm does not collect, store or share personal information about any other person, including viewers and visitors.
  • The owner's OAuth credential is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only for the actions listed above.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any machine-learning model.
  • AstroFarm does not sell TikTok or Google user data.

AstroFarm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

AstroFarm uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

AstroFarm may connect to TikTok using TikTok Login Kit, so that the account holder can sign in with TikTok and authorize AstroFarm. With that explicit authorization, AstroFarm may use TikTok's Content Posting API to upload or publish videos to the authorized account.

  • Permissions requested. The TikTok permissions AstroFarm requests may include basic account information and permission to upload or publish content. AstroFarm only acts on the account of the person who authorized it.
  • How TikTok data is used. TikTok data is used only to provide the integration that was requested: signing in with TikTok and uploading or publishing the account holder's own videos. It is not used for advertising or for any other purpose.
  • Credentials. The OAuth access and refresh credentials issued by TikTok are stored securely on the account holder's own computer and are used only for the actions above. They are not sold and not used for advertising.
  • No sale of data. AstroFarm does not sell TikTok or Google user data.

Use of TikTok is also governed by TikTok's own Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Developer Terms of Service.

Revoking access

The channel owner can withdraw AstroFarm's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately invalidates the stored credential.

For TikTok, the account holder can revoke AstroFarm's authorization at any time through their TikTok account settings. After TikTok access is revoked, the stored TikTok credentials stop working and AstroFarm can no longer access or post to that account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the channel owner through the Star Seeker YouTube channel, or by opening an issue on the project's public GitHub repository at github.com/Tejasvid29/AstroFarm/issues. Issues there are public, so please do not include private information.
