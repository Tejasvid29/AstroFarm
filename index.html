AstroFarm

AstroFarm is a tool for creating, managing, and publishing short-form astrology content. Supported integrations may include YouTube and TikTok.

It is a personal, single-user tool, run by its owner for the owner's own accounts, including the YouTube channel Star Seeker. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and does not collect any information about visitors or viewers.

Before AstroFarm acts on a platform account, it asks the account holder for permission:

  • YouTube: to upload and schedule videos on that one channel through the YouTube API Services.
  • TikTok: to sign in with TikTok Login Kit and, with explicit authorization, to use TikTok's Content Posting API to upload or publish videos to that one account.

The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is accessed and how it is handled, and the Terms of Service explain the rules that apply.
