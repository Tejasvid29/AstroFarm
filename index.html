AstroFarm

AstroFarm is a personal automation tool that produces short astrology videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel, Star Seeker.

It is a single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts, and does not collect any information about visitors or viewers.

To upload and schedule videos, AstroFarm asks the channel owner for permission to manage that one channel through the YouTube API Services. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is handled.
